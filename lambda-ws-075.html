INNER ARCHITECTURE

Anatomy of German Precision.

Click or hover over the structural hotspots below to explore the core components of the Lambda WS 075 system.

01

GFK PA66 Thermal Breaks

24mm–34mm high-performance Polyamide 66 thermal insulation strips reinforced with 25% glass fiber (GFK) to eliminate thermal bridges between indoor and outdoor aluminum frames.

02

Triple EPDM Gasket System

Engineered synthetic EPDM rubber gaskets running continuously around frame and sash perimeters, providing air leakage class 4 and Class E1200 watertightness.

03

Modular Insulation Core

Specialized polyurethane foam inserts inserted inside profile chambers to further decrease convection heat exchange, yielding frame values down to Uf = 0.86 W/m²K.

04

Concealed Euro Groove

Standardized European C-groove profile accepting heavy-duty concealed hinges and multi-point locking gearboxes rated up to 160kg sash weights.

05

52mm Deep Glazing Pocket

Accommodates high-performance double or triple IGU glass panels up to 52mm thickness with warm-edge spacers and Argon gas filling.
