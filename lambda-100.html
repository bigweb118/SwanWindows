100MM MONUMENTAL STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING

Lambda 100 — 100mm Heavy Structural for Perth & WA

Engineered for monumental multi-story glazing, 3.6m tall architectural openings, and extreme coastal wind zones — supporting massive glass sashes up to 400kg with effortless precision.

400kg per sash structural muscle. Built for double-height Perth villas & towers.

With a 100mm profile depth, thickened multi-chamber aluminium webs, and precision CNC stainless roller/hinge systems, Lambda 100 spans huge architectural openings without intermediate steel posts.

  • 100mm High-Inertia Box Section: Deep multi-chamber 6063-T6 structural webs deliver extreme bending resistance across 3.6m spans.
  • Heavy-Duty Reinforced Thermal Break: High-shear PA66 GF25 thermal struts transfer 400kg glass loads safely without creep or sagging.
  • Massive Triple & Jumbo Glass Rebate: Accepts oversized safety, acoustic, and solar-control insulated glass panels up to 72mm thick.
  • 400kg Precision Stainless Gear: Tandem stainless load carriages and reinforced corner cleats keep giant sashes gliding smoothly.
  • Integrated Curtain-Wall Coupling: Direct modular integration into mullion-transom glass facades for seamless commercial/villa elevations.
