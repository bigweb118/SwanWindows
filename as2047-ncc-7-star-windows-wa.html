WA Compliance & Supply-Only Importing — FAQ.

Can owner builders in WA order supply only windows directly from Swan Windows?

Yes. We work directly with WA owner builders, custom home builders and architects. You send your building plans or window schedule, inspect physical corner samples in Perth, approve CAD shop drawings, and receive all-inclusive landed delivery to your site with full compliance documentation.

How far in advance should I order before WA lock-up stage?

Total lead time from CAD sign-off to Perth site delivery is typically 8 to 12 weeks (4–6 weeks factory production & QC + 3–4 weeks sea freight to Fremantle & customs clearance). We recommend finalising your order 10 to 12 weeks before lock-up.
