Why Standard Aluminium Fails in Perth — and Why Thermally Broken Double Glazing Wins.

Standard non-thermally-broken aluminium conducts heat straight through the frame, causing condensation in winter and radiating 40°C summer heat indoors even when paired with double glazing. Our German 75mm and 90mm profiles separate exterior and interior aluminium with 34–54mm glass-fibre reinforced PA66 polyamide thermal breaks and triple EPDM seals.

Choose tilt and turn windows for draft-free top-tilt ventilation and easy indoor cleaning, outward-opening awning and casement windows for coastal driving rain in Cottesloe and Fremantle, or 90mm triple glazed Passive House systems for 8–9 Star NatHERS projects.

Perth Buyer FAQ

Double Glazed Windows Perth — Frequently Asked Questions.

How much do double glazed thermally broken aluminium windows cost in Perth?

Supply only Perth landed pricing typically starts from $460–$620 AUD/m² depending on opening type (fixed, awning, casement or tilt and turn), 75mm vs 90mm thermal break depth and Low-E double vs triple glazing. Use our Instant Price Estimator or send plans for a 48-hour all-inclusive DDP quote.

Are tilt and turn windows suitable for Perth homes?

Yes. Tilt and turn windows seal much tighter than sliding windows against 42°C hot easterlies and winter drafts, allow secure top-tilt ventilation, and open inward for effortless glass cleaning on two-storey homes.

Do your double glazed windows include AS 2047 and NCC 7-Star NatHERS data?

Yes. Every WA order includes AS 2047 performance documentation, AS 1288 safety glazing certificates with permanent glass stamps, and thermal U-value/SHGC data for WA building certifiers and energy assessors.
