FERRALUX® NRWG — Life-Safety Smoke Exhaust for Perth & WA

Certified Natural Smoke and Heat Exhaust Ventilator (NSHEV) with concealed high-speed motorized chain actuators — silently ventilating by day and opening within 60 seconds during fire emergencies.

Concealed 24V twin-chain actuators. Seamless integration into skylights & facades.

Tested as a complete certified unit (profile + actuator + BMS control) to DIN EN 12101-2, FERRALUX® NRWG meets strict commercial atrium, stairwell, and luxury residential fire-engineering codes.

  • Concealed 24V Chain Actuator: Tucked cleanly inside the thermally broken frame with 10,000+ daily ventilation cycles and fail-safe emergency deployment.
  • High-Flow Smoke Exhaust Angle: Opens rapidly up to 60°–90° within 60 seconds to maximize aerodynamic free area (Aa) for smoke clearance.
  • Thermally Broken 75/90mm Frame: Full PA66 thermal break ensures zero heat-loss penalty during normal daily building operation.
  • BMS & Fire-Panel Integration: 24V DC battery-backed control panel links directly to building smoke detectors, wind/rain sensors, and manual break-glass points.
  • 300°C Heat-Resistant Reliability: Certified B300 thermal exposure class guarantees actuator operation even under intense fire heat.
