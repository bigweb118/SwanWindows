How True Soundproof Windows Achieve 45–50 dB Noise Reduction.

Standard symmetrical double glazing (e.g., 4mm + 4mm glass) resonates at the same frequency, letting traffic and aircraft rumble pass through. True acoustic glazing uses asymmetric glass thicknesses (such as 10.76mm acoustic PVB laminated glass + wide argon cavity + 6mm Low-E glass) inside a rigid, triple-gasketed thermally broken frame that eliminates air-leakage sound paths.

With our flagship Lambda WS 090 SilentAir system, you can even enjoy continuous fresh outdoor air in tilted ventilation mode while maintaining remarkable acoustic attenuation — ideal for Perth bedrooms near busy roads or rail lines.

Perth Buyer FAQ

Soundproof Windows Perth — Frequently Asked Questions.

How much quieter is a 45–50 dB soundproof window compared to a standard Perth window?

Because decibels are logarithmic, every 10 dB reduction sounds roughly half as loud to the human ear. Upgrading from a standard ~25 dB single glazed window to a 45–48 dB thermally broken acoustic window reduces perceived outside noise by roughly 75% to 80%.

Does acoustic laminated glass also improve home security and UV protection?

Yes. The acoustic PVB interlayer meets AS 1288 Grade A safety glazing, resists forced entry far better than monolithic glass, and blocks over 99% of harsh Western Australian UV radiation.
