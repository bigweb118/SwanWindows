Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to plan a confident window and door order.

Do you offer supply-only, or do you also install?

Swan Windows specialises in supply-only packages for WA builders, owner-builders and architects — keeping pricing factory-direct and transparent. If you need installation, we can refer independent licensed Perth installers upon request.

How long does importing from China to Perth take?

Typical total lead time is 8–12 weeks from CAD shop drawing sign-off (4–6 weeks factory production + 3–4 weeks sea freight to Fremantle & customs clearance). We recommend ordering 10–12 weeks before lock-up stage.

Are there hidden customs, port or quarantine fees?

No. We provide all-inclusive Perth landed (DDP) quotes covering sea freight, Fremantle port fees, customs clearance, ISPM-15 quarantine-compliant crating and local delivery — with a formal Australian Tax Invoice.

Will the windows pass WA Council & Building Certifier inspection?

Yes. Every order comes with a WA compliance pack: AS 2047 performance documentation & frame labels, AS 1288 safety glazing certificates & permanent glass stamps, plus NatHERS/WERS U-value and SHGC figures for NCC 7-Star energy assessments.

What if glass breaks or hardware is damaged during transit?

All units ship in heavy-duty export steel/wood crates backed by marine transit insurance. We stock common German hardware and EPDM seals locally in Perth and coordinate rapid local glazing replacement if transit damage ever occurs.

Can I inspect physical corner samples in Perth before ordering?

Yes. Contact us via WhatsApp or the form below to book a Perth corner sample viewing — inspect our 75mm/90mm thermal break profiles, Low-E glass samples, powder-coated finishes and German hardware in person.
