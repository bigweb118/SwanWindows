Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to plan a confident window and door order.

Are the windows suitable for Perth conditions?

Yes. Systems can be specified for WA heat, coastal exposure, wind requirements, Low-E glazing and durable powder-coated finishes.

Can you supply only, or arrange installation support?

Both pathways are available. Builders can order supply-only packages, while homeowners can request local measuring, delivery coordination and installer support.

How long does importing from China take?

Lead time depends on drawings, quantity and shipping schedules. We confirm a project timeline after measurement, specification approval and production planning.

What documents can be provided for a project?

Typical documents include shop drawings, glass specifications, hardware schedules, finish colours, warranty terms and relevant testing information.
