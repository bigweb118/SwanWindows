Lift & Slide Doors vs. Standard Sliding & Bifold Doors.

Conventional aluminium sliding doors rely on brush seals that leak drafts, street noise and driving rain, while bifold doors break up views with bulky vertical stiles every 900mm. German lift and slide doors elevate a 3-metre glass panel off its EPDM compression gaskets with a single turn of the handle, gliding effortlessly on precision stainless steel carriages up to 400kg per panel.

Perth Buyer FAQ

Aluminium Sliding Doors Perth — Frequently Asked Questions.

What is the difference between a lift and slide door and a regular stacking sliding door?

A lift and slide door uses a geared handle to lift the sash onto heavy-duty rollers for effortless gliding (up to 400kg per panel) and drops it firmly onto perimeter EPDM gaskets when closed—delivering far superior airtightness, acoustic insulation and wind-driven rain protection than brush-sealed sliding doors.

Can you supply flush zero-threshold tracks for Perth alfresco areas?

Yes. Both our lift and slide doors and A72 door series support recessed zero-barrier thresholds with integrated linear drainage channels, ideal for modern Perth alfresco tiling.
