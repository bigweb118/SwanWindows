ZERO-THRESHOLD LUXURY ENTRANCE & SLIDING DOORS

A72 Door Series — 72mm Premium Door Series for Perth & WA

Seamless indoor-outdoor Perth alfresco living. Featuring a zero-barrier flush threshold, heavy-duty multi-point hook locking, and thermally broken 72mm aluminium frames built for daily family traffic.

Zero-barrier flush threshold. Multi-point stainless hook locks.

Designed for high-traffic Australian entrances, courtyard sliders, and commercial lobbies — A72 combines 200,000+ cycle roller/hinge durability with concealed drainage beneath a wheelchair- and pram-friendly flush sill.

  • Zero-Barrier Flush Threshold: Thermally broken low-profile sill with integrated hidden drainage channel eliminates trip hazards.
  • 3-Point Stainless Hook Locking: Hardened steel hook bolts lock upward and downward into reinforced strike plates for RC3 security.
  • 72mm Thermally Broken Door Profile: Reinforced polyamide strips prevent thermal bowing (bi-metal effect) even under scorching dark-colour sun exposure.
  • Drop-Down Automatic Sill Seal: Lowers silently upon closing to seal drafts, dust, and coastal rain against the flush floor track.
  • Heavy-Duty 250kg Roller / Barrel Hinges: Tested to 200,000+ cycles for effortless fingertip operation on oversized glass doors.
